# Code Of Conduct

Arena welcomes contributors who want to build thoughtful multiplayer games together.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep feedback specific to the work.
- Accept that people have different experience levels.
- Credit ideas and help others understand tradeoffs.
- Prefer clear, practical discussion over personal criticism.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language
- Publishing private information without permission
- Deliberately derailing issues or pull requests
- Spam or low-effort promotional content

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may lead to being blocked from participating in the project.

If you see a problem, contact the repository owner with context and links.
